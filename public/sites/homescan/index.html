Questions

Good questions.

Does HomeScan replace a contractor or inspector?

No. It helps you understand the problem, plan the work and talk to pros with confidence. For safety issues, structural work, electrical, gas or anything that needs a permit, hire a licensed professional.

Is the insurance claim coach legal advice?

No. It helps you document damage and stay organized. It isn’t legal or insurance advice; your policy and your adjuster have the final word.

How accurate are measurements and estimates?

They’re good planning numbers, not a quote. Measurements depend on your phone and conditions; estimates depend on your area and the pros you hire.
