Questions

Good questions.

What can it scan?

Sports cards, Pokémon and other trading card games, comics and magazines, graded slabs, packs and sealed boxes.

Where do the prices come from?

From recent real sales and live market data, refreshed on a schedule. Anything that is an estimate is labelled as one.

Is the AI grade an official grade?

No. The AI Grader and Fake Check are expert-style opinions from your photos to help you decide what to submit or buy. They aren’t a certified grade or authentication.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Base plan is free with 25 scans a week, your collection, sets and DJ. You only pay if you want more.

When can I get it?

ZappHobby is in final review for the App Store, with Google Play right behind it. The buttons on this page will go live the day it does.

Can I delete my account and data?

Anytime, right in the app, or by emailing us. See how to delete your account.

Who makes ZappHobby?

ZappHobby is built by Bizzy Cube LLC in Tucson, Arizona — collectors who got tired of spreadsheets.
