SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

1. Scope

This SMS Privacy Policy describes how BizzyCube LLC and our agency clients collect, use, store, share, and protect information related to SMS text messages. It supplements (and does not replace) our general Privacy Policy at bizzycube.com/privacy.html.

2. Information We Collect

When you opt in to our SMS program, we collect:

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • The date and method of your opt-in (e.g., web form, verbal consent during phone call, reply text).
  • The content of messages you send to us.
  • Metadata about messages we send to you (timestamp, delivery status, carrier).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your phone number and message data solely to:

  • Send appointment confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups related to a service you scheduled.
  • Respond to your direct customer-service questions.
  • Verify your identity for service-related interactions.
  • Maintain compliance records (proof of opt-in/opt-out) as required by law.

4. We Do Not Sell or Share Your Phone Number

BizzyCube does not sell, rent, lease, or share your mobile phone number or SMS conversations with any third party for marketing purposes. Period.

We share your phone number only with:

  • Twilio (our SMS service provider) for the technical purpose of delivering and receiving messages.
  • The specific BizzyCube agency client (e.g., Universal Roofing, Crest Air) whose appointment or service you booked.
  • Law enforcement, only if legally compelled by a valid subpoena or court order.

5. SMS Consent and Service Conditions

SMS consent is never a condition of receiving any product, service, or transaction. You can book appointments, receive service, and complete any transaction with BizzyCube or any of our clients without consenting to receive SMS. SMS is an optional convenience feature.

6. Data Retention

We retain phone numbers and message metadata for as long as you are a customer plus 4 years to comply with legal record-keeping requirements. We retain message content for 90 days unless retention is legally required.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out at any time by replying STOP (immediate and permanent).
  • Request a copy of the SMS data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your phone number and message history (subject to legal retention requirements).

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@bizzycube.com.

8. Security

We protect phone numbers and message content with encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Access is limited to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.

9. Children

Our SMS program is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect SMS consent from minors.

10. Changes

We may update this SMS Privacy Policy. The current version is always at bizzycube.com/sms-privacy.html.
