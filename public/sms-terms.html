1. Program Description

BizzyCube LLC ("BizzyCube," "we," "us") and our agency clients (including but not limited to Universal Roofing LLC, Crest Air LLC, and other small businesses we provide services to) operate this SMS program to send transactional, customer-service, and appointment-related text messages to recipients who have explicitly opted in.

2. Message Types You May Receive

Once you opt in, you may receive the following kinds of SMS messages from us or one of our agency clients:

  • Confirmation of appointments you scheduled by phone, web, or in person.
  • Reminders the day before and ~2 hours before a scheduled appointment.
  • Replies to questions you sent us by SMS.
  • Follow-up after a service visit (e.g., feedback request, scope changes).
  • Customer service responses to your direct messages.

We do not send marketing or promotional SMS through this program. If we ever launch a marketing program, it will be governed by a separate opt-in.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your interactions with us. A typical customer receives 2-5 messages per appointment (confirmation, reminders, follow-up). You will not receive more than 10 messages per week unless you are actively in conversation with us.

4. Charges

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge you to receive SMS from us; standard carrier rates apply per your mobile plan.

5. How to Opt In

You opt in to receive SMS in one of these ways:

  • By replying YES to a confirmation text from us after we schedule an appointment for you.
  • By entering your phone number on a web form on our or our client's website and checking the optional SMS consent box.
  • By texting any keyword (such as START, YES, or SUBSCRIBE) to one of our numbers.

SMS consent is never required to use our services or our clients' services. You can complete any transaction without agreeing to receive SMS.

6. How to Opt Out

You can stop receiving SMS at any time by replying with any of: STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, END, CANCEL, or QUIT. We will send one final confirmation that you have been opted out, and you will receive no further messages from us. To resume, reply START or SUBSCRIBE.

7. Help

For help, reply HELP or INFO to any of our messages. You can also reach us at support@bizzycube.com.
